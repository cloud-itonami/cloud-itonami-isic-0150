# Governance

Maintained by the cloud-itonami org. Decisions land as ADRs in the
superproject ledger. The actor pattern (advisor-LLM sealed behind an
independent governor, append-only audit ledger) is non-negotiable per
ADR-2607011000: the governor gates every action; direct field/animal-
handling equipment operation, finalizing a pesticide-application decision,
and slaughter/culling decisions are permanently blocked; any non-
`:propose` effect is permanently blocked; `:high`/`:safety-critical`
actions (crop or animal health/welfare concerns, high-cost supply orders)
require human sign-off.
